## 3. Frozen HSA Visual Direction

### A — Weave Thread / Node

The Thread/Node represents the relationship between a meaningful point in the original document and the resulting understanding.

Requirements:

- Thread originates from a meaningful document point.
- Thread remains thin and restrained.
- Node is small, flat, matte, and non-character-like.
- Node must not become an orb, avatar, face, button, badge, or decorative AI object.
- No neon, holographic, sci-fi, or excessive glow treatment.

### C — Document Marginalia

Marginalia provides a calm annotation beside the relevant document point.

Requirements:

- Commentary, not replacement for the original document.
- Visually subordinate to the document.
- Identifies the part of the document that matters.
- Supports the document → understanding relationship.
- Remains low-density and calm.

## 4. Frozen Human + AI Relationship

AI is represented as **understanding and connection**, not as a person.

The HSA direction must not represent AI as a face, avatar, humanoid, robot, physical hand, mascot, or holographic character.

The human remains the protagonist and decision-maker.

**Core principle:** AI suggests. Human decides.

## 5. Frozen HSA Environment

The emotional frame is:

- real human home
- natural daylight
- warm physical materials
- wood, paper, ceramic, textile
- normal everyday computer/laptop
- family or human life as secondary context

The home environment remains visually warmer and more human than the digital layer.

## 6. Frozen Weave Relationship

The HSA visual language uses the approved Weave production vocabulary:

- deep petrol / dark teal grounding
- restrained copper/gold accents
- Thread/Node relationship
- calm typography and spacing
- document-as-node / understanding-as-thread principle

HSA-08 does not create a second visual language for AI.

## 7. Frozen Storyline

**Document → Point → Explanation → Confidence → Suggested Action → Human Decision**

This is the primary visual narrative.

## 8. AI Transparency and Confidence

Confidence is expressed in plain language, such as **“Mostly confident.”**

No fake-precise percentage is part of the frozen direction.

The original document remains authoritative. AI explanation is commentary.

Source/grounding and confidence remain separate concepts.

## 9. AI-Origin Marker

A consistent AI-origin marker identifies AI-generated/suggested text.

It is separate in meaning from:

- Thread/Node — connection/understanding
- Confidence — certainty

These functions must not visually collapse into one AI symbol.

## 10. What Is NOT Frozen

The following are explicitly not authoritative:

- generated names, dates, institutions, or document text
- exact mockup copy
- exact button labels
- exact screen layout
- generated logos or marks
- exact photography
- mockup/editor chrome
- demo statistics
- specific sample documents

The freeze concerns **principles and visual language**, not generated reference-image content.

## 11. Accessibility and Localization

The freeze does not claim that accessibility or localization has already been technically validated.
