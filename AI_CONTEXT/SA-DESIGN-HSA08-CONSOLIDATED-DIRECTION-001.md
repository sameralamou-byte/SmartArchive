## 1. Purpose

This record consolidates the approved HSA-08 visual-direction decision and the independent refinement findings.

HSA-08 is a controlled refinement of HSA-07. It is a visual-direction reference only and is not a production UI specification.

---

## 2. Founder Decision

The Founder approved the combined:

### A — Weave Thread / Node

and

### C — Document Marginalia / Annotation

as the HSA-08 working direction.

The purpose is to represent SmartArchive intelligence without a humanoid AI character.

---

## 3. Core HSA-08 Principle

> **Real person → real document → SmartArchive understands → relevant point is connected → explanation → confidence → suggested action → human decides.**

SmartArchive should appear through understanding and connection, not as another person in the room.

---

## 4. A — Weave Thread / Node

The Thread:

- remains inside the digital experience
- originates from a meaningful point in the original document
- uses restrained petrol/deep-teal as the base language
- may use restrained copper/gold at the Node
- remains thin and calm
- must have semantic meaning
- must not become decorative technology

The Node:

- is small
- is non-character-like
- is not a button, badge, mascot, or glowing orb
- represents the point where document understanding connects to explanation

Avoid:

- neon
- holographic effects
- excessive glow
- sci-fi motion
- technical network density

---

## 5. C — Document Marginalia / Annotation

The annotation:

- is attached to the relevant document point
- feels like a calm margin note
- explains the document in plain language
- remains commentary, not replacement of the original
- should not resemble an alert or marketing pop-up

Example conceptual wording:

> “Mostly confident — this letter says you must register by 18 August.”

This example is conceptual only and is not approved production copy.

---

## 6. What Remains from HSA-07

### Physical / emotional environment

- warm real home
- natural daylight
- real human presence
- family as secondary context
- wood
- paper
- ceramic
- textile
- calm and trustworthy atmosphere

### Digital environment

- normal laptop/computer
- deep petrol / dark-teal Weave language
- restrained copper/gold accents
- document-first hierarchy
- plain-language confidence
- human-control framing

### Product philosophy

- original document remains authoritative
- AI explanation is commentary
- AI suggests
- human decides

---

## 7. What Changes from HSA-07

Remove:

- humanoid AI face
- AI avatar
- digital human figure
- AI hand
- robot
- mascot
- silhouette representing AI

Replace with:

- Weave Thread / Node
- document-anchored marginalia
- quiet explanation relationship

---

## 8. Human + AI Relationship

The visual hierarchy must remain:

**Human → Document → Understanding → Suggested Action → Human Decision**

The AI must not become the visual protagonist.

SmartArchive should feel like calm assistance embedded in the document experience.

---

## 9. Document-First Rule

The original document remains the source of authority.

The visual must prioritize:

1. original document
2. relevant source point
3. explanation
4. confidence
5. source/original access
6. suggested action
7. human decision

The Thread and annotation exist to clarify this relationship.

---

## 10. Confidence and Transparency

Use plain-language confidence.

Preferred conceptual treatment:

- “Mostly confident”
- bar/visual indicator where appropriate
- source/original access

Do not use:

- fake precision
- invented percentages
- fabricated statistics
- unsupported certainty

A future concept should demonstrate uncertainty where appropriate.

---

## 11. AI-Origin Marker

AI-generated text must retain the project's single consistent AI-origin marker.

The marker is a governance indicator.

It must not become:

- an AI character
- a mascot
- a decorative badge
- a second AI visual language

---

## 12. One Primary Storyline

HSA production experiences remain low-density.

The visual reference must not be interpreted as permission to build a multi-dashboard screen.

The intended story is:

> **What is this document saying, what matters, and what can I choose to do next?**

---

## 13. Visual Language

### Retain

- warm home
- natural daylight
- real materials
- real human context
- petrol/deep teal
- restrained copper/gold
- calm typography
- subtle Thread/Node
- document-grounded annotation

### Avoid

- neon purple/blue
- cyberpunk
- holograms
- futuristic rooms
- glowing hardware
- floating screens
- robots
- avatars
- AI faces
- special hardware
- smart-home dashboards

---

## 14. Content Governance

Reference images are not authoritative data sources.

Do not copy:

- real personal names
- real family names
- real institutions
- real company names
- government seals
- exact dates unless deliberately approved
- fabricated document counts
- fabricated statistics
- pricing
- legal/privacy claims
- generated logos or marks
- unapproved feature claims

Use fictional/demo-safe content when a visual candidate requires text.

---

## 15. Domain Restrictions

Do not introduce unapproved demonstrations involving:

- medical/health analysis
- insurance analytics
- financial prediction
- neural CPU claims
- other invented technical capabilities

A visual reference must not create a new product requirement.

---

## 16. Accessibility and Localization

Static imagery cannot prove:

- WCAG compliance
- keyboard navigation
- focus behavior
- responsive behavior
- Arabic layout
- RTL behavior
- German/English localization

These remain later implementation-validation requirements.

Language badges do not constitute proof of RTL or accessibility.

---

## 17. Controlled Refinement Rule

The next candidate should change only the agreed HSA-07 AI representation.

Do not use the next candidate to introduce:

- new features
- new architecture
- new hardware
- new product flows
- new pricing
- new AI capabilities
- new integrations
- new business claims

---

## 18. HSA-08 Acceptance Criteria

A candidate is aligned with this direction when:

- human remains the protagonist
- home remains warm and calm
- original document remains central
- AI remains inside the computer
- no humanoid AI representation exists
- Thread originates from a meaningful document point
- Node remains subtle and non-character-like
- marginalia is limited and meaningful
- confidence is plain-language
- AI-origin marking is consistent
- human control remains explicit
- digital layer remains visually secondary to the home
- no sci-fi/neon/holographic language is introduced
- demo data is fictional/safe
