## 4. Core Concept to Preserve

The strongest idea in HSA-06 is:

> **A real person reaches toward SmartArchive, and SmartArchive reaches back to help — while the person remains in control.**

This supports:

> **Human in control. AI here to help.**

The concept should remain human-first.

The AI must not visually become a replacement for the human, a robot, a mascot, or an autonomous character.

## 5. Mandatory Refinements

### 5.1 Human–AI relationship

**Current issue:** The AI hand and glowing contact point can become the dominant visual focus and may read as a generic AI-marketing trope.

**Required refinement:**

- Keep the human hand physically grounded and dominant.
- Reduce the AI hand's visual dominance.
- Keep AI representation translucent and secondary.
- Reduce the contact glow.
- Consider expressing the connection more strongly through a restrained Weave thread/node language.
- Do not introduce a robot or AI character.

**Decision status:** Mandatory refinement.

---

### 5.2 Confidence representation

**Current issue:** The image displays `92%`.

This conflicts with the established SmartArchive confidence principle.

**Required refinement:**

Replace percentage-based confidence with a plain-language representation such as:

- High confidence
- Mostly confident
- Needs review

The exact final wording must follow the existing approved product language.

**Decision status:** Mandatory refinement.

---

### 5.3 One primary storyline

**Current issue:** The current laptop composition visually combines:

- Home
- Document Understanding
- AI Assistant
- Timeline
- File Locker
- additional overlays

This makes the image a product poster rather than a calm HSA storyline.

**Required refinement:**

Reduce the visual hierarchy to one primary moment.

Recommended primary storyline:

```text
REAL DOCUMENT
      ↓
WHAT IT MEANS
      ↓
CONFIDENCE / SOURCE
      ↓
WHAT TO DO
      ↓
REMINDER
```

Other capabilities may remain conceptually present but must not compete with the primary storyline.

**Decision status:** Mandatory refinement.

---

### 5.4 Fictional demonstration data

The current image contains:

- “Samer”
- “Ahmad”
- “Mainova”
- “Allianz”
- exact document counts
- exact confidence percentage
- specific dates

These must not appear in the refined reference.

**Required refinement:**

Use clearly fictional or generic demonstration content.

Do not imply:

- real customer data
- real vendor relationships
- real endorsements
- real family data
- real statistics

**Decision status:** Mandatory refinement.

---

### 5.5 File Locker

The File Locker concept is valuable as a metaphor for organized personal/family documents.

**Keep the principle:**

- many documents
- organized
- accessible
- connected to understanding/action

**Do not imply:**

- a physical SmartArchive hardware product
- invented storage capacity
- fabricated counts
- unapproved folder taxonomy

**Decision status:** Retain concept, refine visual expression.

---

### 5.6 Lighting

The current image reads as warm evening/golden-hour lighting.

This is attractive, but HSA-01 established a calm real-home direction with natural light.

**Required refinement:**

Move the visual closer to natural, calm domestic light while preserving warmth.

The goal is:

> warm real home + calm daylight + restrained digital layer.

Evening mood may remain a secondary exploration later, but it should not become the default HSA visual identity without separate approval.

**Decision status:** Recommended refinement / current HSA alignment priority.
