## 5. Controlled Change — AI Representation

### Current

The laptop contains a glowing humanoid AI face/profile.

### Problem

Independent reviewers identified this as the main remaining conflict:

- it can read as an AI avatar/character
- it can make AI appear like a separate entity
- it conflicts with the preferred Weave abstract-intelligence language
- it weakens the human-as-protagonist relationship
- it risks creating a permanent AI-person visual language

### Required change

Remove the humanoid AI face.

Replace it with:

- a subtle Thread/Node intelligence representation
- a restrained connection between document, explanation and action
- no face
- no robot
- no mascot
- no human-like AI character
- no dramatic hologram

The AI should feel **present through understanding**, not present as a person.

---

## 6. Human + AI Relationship

The refined concept should communicate:

> **Human in control. AI here to help.**

The visual hierarchy should be:

```text
HUMAN
  ↓
DOCUMENT
  ↓
SMARTARCHIVE UNDERSTANDING
  ↓
SUGGESTED ACTION
  ↓
HUMAN DECISION
```

AI must not visually become the protagonist.

## 7. Confidence / Transparency

A future concept should also demonstrate an uncertainty state where appropriate.
