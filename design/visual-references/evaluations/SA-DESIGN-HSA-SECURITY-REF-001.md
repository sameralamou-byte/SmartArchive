## 4. Content note — do not silently promote to a factual claim

"Protected," "Under your control," "Access only by you," "Safe for the future," "Your Privacy. Your Choice." are all outcome/promise language, not technical claims. **Do not replace any of these with a specific mechanism (encryption method, certification, compliance standard) unless it is independently verified true** — same rule as `SA-DESIGN-ESA-SITE-SPECIFICATION-001` §4.5 already states for ESA's Security page, and for the same reason: this is the one class of content where an unverified claim is a real trust/legal risk, not just a taste question.
