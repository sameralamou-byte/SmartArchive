## 1. What this document is

This records a deliberate exception to `SmartArchive_Master_Project_Context_v1.0.md`'s shared-DNA rules, for **ESA (Enterprise) only**. HSA is unaffected and remains governed by the existing HSA-08 freeze (`SA-DESIGN-HSA08-FREEZE-001`). Do not apply anything in this document to HSA.

The Founder has stated there is a later document establishing this exception that does not survive on disk — searched `documentation/`, `AI_CONTEXT/`, and the ESA restoration package on 2026-09-20, no written trace found. This record exists so the decision has a durable, checkable home going forward, sourced from the Founder's direct statement in this session rather than a recovered original.

## 2. The exception, stated plainly

ESA is a business-only product. It does not need to carry human warmth, comfort, or the "AI suggests, human decides via a connection-not-a-face" softening that HSA's frozen direction requires. The agreed ESA visual language is **AI/machines, future, and space/holographic** — explicitly different from HSA's human-centered Weave direction, not a variation of it.

This directly supersedes, for ESA only, these specific lines from the master doc:
- Section 11 ("HSA and ESA must clearly look like the same product family... not two unrelated design systems") — no longer applies to ESA's visual treatment.
- Section 13's locked Weave palette (petrol/dark-teal + copper/gold) — ESA uses a different, deliberately chosen palette instead (see below).
- Section 20/64's "AI suggests, human decides" framing as a *human-comfort* device — the AI-as-architecture treatment below is kept, but for a different reason (see Section 4).

Everything else in the master doc not specifically named here still applies to ESA (confidence rules, source/grounding rules, honesty about fake data, no fabricated functionality, accessibility, DE/EN/AR + RTL).

## 3. Approved palette

Deep blue / midnight-navy base with magenta/neon-pink accents throughout. This is intentionally different from HSA's petrol/copper palette — the two products are not meant to look like the same family for ESA's marketing surfaces.

## 4. AI representation — kept from the master doc, for a different reason

The master doc's ban on "glowing AI faces, humanoid AI, purple/blue gradients as a generic AI signal" was written partly to protect human comfort. That reason doesn't apply to ESA anymore. But a *second*, independent reason survives: a literal giant AI face reads as a generic, dated "AI hype" visual cliché to an enterprise buyer, undermining ESA's own stated experience principle (*Powerful · Precise · Analytical · Controlled · Traceable · Governed*). Enterprise/AI products that skew toward this positioning (Palantir, Databricks) lean on abstract network/data visualization rather than character-like AI, specifically for buyer credibility.

**Decision:** intelligence in ESA imagery is represented as an ambient network of glowing points and threads woven into architecture and surfaces — never a face, avatar, humanoid figure, or character. This is authorized for the same reason the older rule existed originally, just not the human-warmth reason.
