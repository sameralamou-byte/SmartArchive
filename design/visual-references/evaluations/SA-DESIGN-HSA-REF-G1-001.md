---

## 1. Governance Status

The five Group 1 images are **candidate visual references only**.

No image is currently an approved SmartArchive design.

The agreed process is:

**Collect → Independently Evaluate → Compare → Improve/Synthesize → Final Evaluation → Founder Vote → Design Freeze → Implementation**

A final direction requires a minimum of **4 YES votes out of 5**.

A winning direction may subsequently be improved, but its fundamental approved direction must not be silently replaced.

---

## 2. Evaluation Principle

The objective is not to select the prettiest image.

The selected direction must be capable of becoming a coherent HSA visual language supporting:

- Home
- Documents
- Document Understanding
- AI Assistant
- Actions
- Reminders
- Timeline
- Settings

while remaining recognizably one SmartArchive product.

Evaluation considers:

1. SmartArchive identity
2. HSA suitability
3. Human-centered experience
4. Trust and emotional comfort
5. Document-first communication
6. AI/intelligence expression
7. Information hierarchy
8. Visual clarity
9. Accessibility/readability
10. Multilingual and RTL suitability
11. Responsive/web adaptability
12. Architecture/design-governance alignment

# 6. Current Cross-Reviewer Observation

The reviews are intentionally **not averaged yet**.

The strongest current convergence is:

### HSA-02 — Family / Real Documents

It is the strongest candidate in the ChatGPT and Reviewer 3 assessments, while Claude also identified the underlying human/document storytelling as the most valuable material, although Claude scored the supplied execution much more strictly.

This means HSA-02 should be treated as a **leading candidate for further discussion**, NOT as an approved design.

A second important pattern is that the reviewers distinguish between:

**visual/storytelling inspiration**

and

**actual UI/design language**.

This distinction must remain in the final decision process.

---

# 7. Emerging HSA Design Principles

These are observations from the current reviews, not yet frozen design decisions.

Potentially valuable principles:

- Human first.
- Documents should be meaningful, not decorative.
- AI should assist rather than dominate the visual experience.
- Real-life document situations are stronger than abstract AI spectacle.
- Family and multi-generational use can be relevant to HSA.
- Calm environments are preferable to intimidating technology environments.
- Generated/fabricated UI text must never be treated as production content.
- Consequential actions require clear user control.
- A candidate's visual chrome should not automatically be adopted merely because its storytelling is useful.

# 9. Remaining Review Steps

1. Obtain Reviewer 4 independent evaluation.
2. Obtain Founder evaluation.
3. Compare all five perspectives.
4. Identify agreements and disagreements.
5. Separate taste disagreements from product/governance conflicts.
6. Select the strongest candidate(s) for synthesis.
7. Modify/improve the selected direction.
8. Re-evaluate the improved direction.
9. Conduct final vote.
10. Require minimum **4 YES / 5** for design freeze.
11. Produce the final HSA visual reference package.
12. Only then authorize implementation.

---

# 10. Implementation Gate

Until the HSA visual direction is formally frozen:

**DO NOT:**
- implement the final HSA UI
- redesign the repository around one candidate
- create production components based on an unapproved reference
- silently adopt colors, typography or imagery from a candidate
- treat generated text/data in reference images as real product content

The purpose of this process is to avoid major redesign later.
