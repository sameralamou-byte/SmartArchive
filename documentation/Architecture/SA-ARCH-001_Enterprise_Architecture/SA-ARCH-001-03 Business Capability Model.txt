1. Executive Summary
The SmartArchive Business Capability Model defines the complete set of business capabilities required to operate, manage, and continuously evolve the SmartArchive Enterprise Platform.
It provides a structured view of what the business does, independent of organizational structure, software implementation, or technology choices. The model establishes a common business language for stakeholders, architects, developers, and business owners.
The Business Capability Model serves as the foundation for enterprise architecture planning, solution design, investment prioritization, digital transformation initiatives, and capability maturity assessment across the SmartArchive platform.
The capabilities described in this document represent the core functional areas that enable SmartArchive to deliver intelligent document management, AI-powered automation, enterprise governance, security, compliance, analytics, integrations, and future platform growth.
This document also establishes the relationships between business capabilities and the supporting application, data, AI, infrastructure, and security architectures to ensure alignment across the entire SmartArchive Enterprise Architecture.
The Business Capability Model shall be used as the primary business architecture reference for strategic planning, solution development, capability assessment, and future enterprise expansion.

2. Purpose
The purpose of this document is to define the Business Capability Model for the SmartArchive Enterprise Platform and establish a common understanding of the business functions required to achieve the platform's strategic objectives.
This document identifies the core business capabilities that enable SmartArchive to deliver intelligent document management, Artificial Intelligence services, workflow automation, enterprise governance, security, compliance, analytics, and digital business operations.
The Business Capability Model provides a business-focused view of the enterprise that is independent of organizational structure, technology implementation, and software architecture. It serves as the foundation for business architecture, enterprise architecture, solution design, capability planning, and future platform evolution.
This document supports:

• Strategic planning and investment prioritization. 
• Business process improvement and digital transformation.

5. Business Capability Model
The SmartArchive Business Capability Model provides a structured representation of the business capabilities required to achieve the strategic objectives of the SmartArchive Enterprise Platform. It organizes enterprise functionality into logical business domains that remain stable regardless of technology, implementation approach, or organizational changes.
The capability model establishes a common business language that enables effective communication between business stakeholders, enterprise architects, solution architects, product owners, developers, and operational teams.
Business capabilities are organized into hierarchical levels to support enterprise planning, architecture governance, capability maturity assessment, investment prioritization, and continuous business improvement.
5.1 Capability Hierarchy
The SmartArchive Business Capability Model is organized into three hierarchical levels:
LevelDescriptionLevel 1Enterprise Business Domains representing the highest-level business capabilities.Level 2Major Business Capabilities within each business domain.Level 3Detailed Business Functions and services that implement each capability.This hierarchical structure enables business planning at both strategic and operational levels while maintaining full traceability across the Enterprise Architecture.
5.2 Business Capability Principles
The SmartArchive Business Capability Model follows these guiding principles:
• Business capabilities are technology-independent. 
• Every capability delivers measurable business value. 
• Capabilities are reusable across multiple business processes. 
• Capabilities are aligned with enterprise strategy. 
• AI capabilities are integrated as core business capabilities. 
• Business capabilities support multilingual operations. 
• Security and compliance are embedded across all capability domains. 
• Business capabilities evolve through continuous improvement and governance.

7.3 Capability Dependency Principles
Business capability dependencies shall comply with the following architectural principles:
• Dependencies shall be clearly defined and documented. 
• Circular business dependencies shall be avoided. 
• Business capabilities shall remain loosely coupled. 
• Shared services shall be reusable across multiple capability domains. 
• AI capabilities shall support, not replace, core business capabilities. 
• Security and governance shall apply across all capability domains. 
• Business capability interactions shall remain technology-independent.
