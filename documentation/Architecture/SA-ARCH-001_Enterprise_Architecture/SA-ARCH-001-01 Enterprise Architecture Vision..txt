# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Enterprise Architecture of the SmartArchive platform.

It establishes the overall architectural vision, guiding principles, architectural layers, stakeholders, system boundaries, and strategic objectives that govern the design, implementation, deployment, operation, and future evolution of the platform.

This document serves as the primary architectural reference for every engineering domain within the SmartArchive repository.

---

# 2. Scope

This architecture applies to the complete SmartArchive platform, including but not limited to:

- Enterprise Architecture
- Artificial Intelligence
- API Services
- Database Architecture
- Security
- Infrastructure
- DevOps
- Frontend
- Backend
- Integrations
- Marketplace
- Operations
- Governance
- Compliance
- Analytics
- User Experience

The architecture covers both current capabilities and future platform evolution.

---

# 3. Objectives

The Enterprise Architecture aims to:

- Establish a single architectural vision.
- Provide a scalable enterprise platform.
- Enable AI-first document intelligence.
- Support multilingual document processing.
- Ensure security by design.
- Enable cloud-native deployment.
- Support modular development.
- Simplify maintenance and future enhancements.
- Enable enterprise integrations.
- Maintain complete architectural traceability.

# 5. Executive Summary

SmartArchive is an AI-powered enterprise document intelligence platform designed for individuals, small businesses, enterprises, and public organizations.

The platform extends traditional document management by combining intelligent document understanding, AI-assisted automation, multilingual capabilities, semantic search, workflow orchestration, and enterprise integration into a unified architecture.

Rather than replacing existing ERP, CRM, or business systems, SmartArchive acts as an intelligent document layer that enhances existing business processes.

---

# 6. Vision

To become the world's most intelligent enterprise document platform by combining artificial intelligence, automation, security, and enterprise architecture into a scalable cloud-native solution.

---

# 7. Mission

Deliver an enterprise-grade document intelligence platform capable of securely managing, understanding, classifying, searching, and automating documents across every business domain.

---

# 8. Architectural Goals

The architecture shall:

- Support millions of documents.
- Support multiple organizations.
- Support multilingual users.
- Be AI-ready.
- Be cloud-native.
- Be modular.
- Be secure by design.
- Support high availability.
- Enable enterprise integrations.
- Support continuous delivery.
